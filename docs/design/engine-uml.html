1. Order and position lifecycles

Order
Position and account

gap: the strategy is not told, or the state is missing from the code

Every order transition maps to a method, but only Filled reaches the strategy (on_fill). Rejected, Canceled and Expired are silent. The account has no Liquidated state: equity at or below zero keeps trading.

2. One bar of the simulation

A protective stop created mid-bar only sees the points after the entry. The order of the two middle points decides a same-bar stop-out. This is the diagram that would have shown today's bug before it shipped.

5. Gaps

#GapMoves today's numbers
G1No zero-equity rule. Equity can go below zero and the pair keeps trading at full notional. Sizing ignores current equity, so leverage grows as losses mount. Cash accounts can spend cash they don't have.yes, books 27-29, 34-36, 40-41
G2Orphan stop. A stop-loss is not linked to its position. If another order closes the position, the stop stays live and can open a reverse trade, even in a long-only strategy.no, latent
G3Silent order outcomes. A rejected, canceled or expired entry is never reported, so a strategy can wait on it forever.no, latent
G4Unprotected position. Cancelling all orders also cancels the stop-loss.no, latent
G5Fill times on bar fills. Stamped at the bar's start, up to a full bar early. Prices are right; times and durations are not.times only
G6Unstated assumptions. No costs (intentional), all-or-nothing fills at any size, stop fill rule.documentation

6. Requirements these imply

#RequirementCloses
R1The simulator shall stop a pair from opening or adding to positions when its equity is at or below zero.G1
R2Closing a position shall cancel every protective stop attached to it.G2, G4
R3The strategy shall be notified of every terminal order outcome: filled, rejected, canceled, expired.G3
R4Reported fill times shall be the time the price was reached, to the resolution of the fill data.G5
R5The report shall state its execution assumptions: costs, fill size, stop fill rule.G6
